The Story Behind The Code

From Kalutara to the world: 8 years of crafting digital excellence

Hello, I'm Dinusha

My journey began in the coastal town of Kalutara, Sri Lanka, where I discovered my passion for the web at a young age. What started as curiosity about how websites work evolved into a lifelong dedication to creating digital experiences that matter.

For the past 8 years, I've been bridging the gap between beautiful design and technical excellence. I believe that a website should not only look stunning but should also be built with clean, semantic code that search engines love and users find intuitive.

My work has taken me across continents, collaborating with award-winning agencies in Belgium, Sweden, Australia, and the UK. This global exposure has given me unique insights into diverse market needs and cutting-edge web technologies.

As a Zend PHP 7 Certified Engineer, I bring enterprise-level expertise to every project. But beyond the certifications, what truly drives me is solving complex problems and helping businesses establish their digital presence with websites that are fast, accessible, and optimized for success.
